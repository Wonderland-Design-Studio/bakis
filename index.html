ABOUT US

A supplier of Electrical and Mechanical commodities

At Bakis Engineering, we aim to develop tailor made solutions that would meet our customer’s needs and provide sustainable benefits and create a shared value

Bakis Engineering (Pty) Ltd was founded in November 2016 and is 100% Black Women Owned (BWO), and a level 1 BBBEE, Engineering Company.

Our core business is the supply of Electrical and Mechanical commodities.

VISION

“To offer customers world class solutions that adds value and gives them the competitive edge”

Mission

We aim to forge close and lasting relationships with our customers, attain the highest quality in our materials and service delivery, attain effective profit while maintaining customer satisfaction, and prioritize being sustainable.

Values

We prioritize customer focus by anticipating and satisfying their needs, pursue excellence through high professional standards, prioritize safety by adopting best practices, uphold integrity by being consistently honest and transparent, and foster teamwork in our supportive environment.
